## ⚠️ First, the real daily cost
Meta India adds **18% GST** on top of ad spend. So:
```
 ₹1,200/day media  →  billed ₹1,416/day  (₹1,200 + ₹216 GST)
```
₹6,000 of actual cash = **~4.2 days of PURE burn** if you sell nothing.

**But you won't sell nothing** — and that's the whole point. ₹6k isn't "runway," it's **ignition money**: enough to run until the first advances refill the wallet. Your tested CAC (₹450) means the first sale usually lands Day 1-2 — before ₹6k runs dry.

---

## 🔁 The flywheel (why ₹6k stretches way past 4 days)
Your rule: **50% advance AT BOOKING, before you build.** Each sale puts cash back in the wallet the same day.
```
 ₹2,000 starter  → ₹1,000 advance now + ₹1,000 on delivery
 ₹4,999 business → ₹2,500 advance now + ₹2,500 on delivery
```
At ₹1,200/day and CAC ₹450-700, you can close **1.7 – 2.7 sales/day**. Even ONE starter a day nearly pays the whole ad bill back.

## 🛡️ The 4 rules that make ₹6,000 actually work
1. **50% advance at booking, every time.** This is the ignition. Skip it and ₹6k = 4 flat days.
2. **Day 3-4 checkpoint:** spent ~₹4-5k and got **<2 total sales?** → CAC is blown. **Pause, fix the offer/creative** before burning the rest. (Don't pour good money after bad.)
3. **Keep a tiny GST buffer.** A declined charge on a 14-day-old account is a ban trigger. Never let the card fail.
4. **Don't touch the ₹15k reserve.** ₹6k is ad ignition; the reserve stays sacred (master-plan rule).

## 🟡 One caution on the new account
Best practice is NOT to jump a brand-new account straight to ₹1,200/day (ban + learning risk). If the account is fresh, consider **₹600-800/day for Days 1-3, then ₹1,200**. If you're set on ₹1,200 from Day 1, watch closely for payment declines and erratic-spend flags.
