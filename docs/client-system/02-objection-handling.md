# 24dev — Objection-Handling Playbook

> WhatsApp-ready scripts for skeptical, price-sensitive Indian buyers.
> Copy-paste, tweak the name, send. Every reply is honesty-guarded — nothing here violates the guardrails.

**How to use this doc**
- Each objection = the **exact line a buyer will type** + your **word-for-word reply**.
- Keep replies short. On WhatsApp, a wall of text reads as "salesy/desperate." 2–4 short lines wins.
- Always close on an **outcome** (more customers, found on Google, trust) — never on "cheap."
- When you can, attach the **live demo link** instead of arguing. A working URL beats every sentence.

**The one wedge to lead with everywhere:** *Hosting free for life on YOUR own account — no year-2 renewal trap.* This is the single thing the ₹999 crowd cannot match. Bring it up early and often.

## 1. "Why is it so cheap? What's the catch?"

> **Buyer:** "₹2,000 only? Itna sasta? What's the catch — koi hidden charge toh nahi?"

**Reply:**
```
Fair question 🙂 No catch — here's exactly why it's this price:

It's just me + a fast, modern build stack + AI doing the heavy lifting.
No big agency office, no sales team, no middlemen — so you don't pay for any of that.

You get the same outcome a ₹15,000 agency gives: a clean site that brings you customers and makes your business look trustworthy.

Everything's in writing before you pay a rupee. Nothing hidden.
```

**Why this works:** reframes price as *process* (lean operation + AI), never as "budget." Outcome-led close. Pre-empts the hidden-charge fear in the same breath.

## 2. "I'm getting it for ₹999 elsewhere."

> **Buyer:** "Bro one company is giving website for ₹999. Why should I pay you ₹2,000?"

**Reply:**
```
I know exactly the ones you mean — and ₹999 is rarely ₹999. Here's the usual fine print:

• "Free domain" = a .co.in nobody types. The real .com costs +₹799 extra.
• Hosting is "free year 1" — then ₹1,499/yr forever. That's a subscription leash.
• Capped at 3 pages. 4th page? ₹500 each.
• The site sits on THEIR server — you can't leave without losing it.

24dev:
✅ Real .com included
✅ Hosting FREE for life — on YOUR own account, no year-2 bill
✅ You own everything — fire me anytime, the site keeps running
✅ Full scope written down upfront, no per-page traps

You're not paying ₹1,000 more. You're avoiding ₹1,500/year forever. 🙂
```

**Why this works:** names the competitor's exact decoy mechanics (all verified) so you're the honest option. Converts a price gap into a *cost-over-time* win. Honest framing is itself the trust signal.

> **Short version (if they're impatient):**
> ```
> ₹999 ones charge +₹799 for the real .com, then ₹1,499/yr hosting forever, and cap you at 3 pages.
> Mine: real .com + hosting free for life on your own account + you own 100% of it. No year-2 surprise.
> ```

## 3. "Show me your work — what have you done before?"

> **Buyer:** "Can you show me some websites you've made? Any portfolio?"

**Reply:**
```
Honestly — I'd rather show you something better than a portfolio of someone else's site.

Here's a live demo so you can click around yourself: [demo link]

And here's the deal that protects you: you pay just 50% to start, and the balance only AFTER your site is live and you've seen it working. So you're never paying for a promise — you pay for a result you can see.

Want me to build a quick sample of YOUR homepage first? Then you decide.
```

**Why this works:** honesty guardrail — no fake testimonials. Substitutes *demonstrable* trust (live demo + pay-on-delivery + optional sample build) for social proof you don't have yet. The 50/50 structure reframed as *buyer protection* defuses the no-portfolio risk.

> **Note to self:** keep 1–2 live demo URLs ready at all times. A clickable link closes this objection instantly. If you've done even one real client, ask permission to share it as a reference — one contactable reference outweighs ten screenshots.

## 4. "Can I pay fully after delivery?"

> **Buyer:** "I'll pay the full amount once the site is ready. Advance nahi de sakta."

**Reply:**
```
Totally understand — trust goes both ways 🙂

The way I keep it fair for both of us: 50% to start, 50% once it's live and you're happy. I can't put days of work in on 0% — but I've made sure you never risk the full amount.

Payment is through Razorpay (proper invoice + receipt, not a personal UPI), so it's on record and protected. You see real progress before the second half.

That 50/50 split is YOUR safety net as much as mine.
```

**Why this works:** holds the 50% line (non-negotiable for solo cashflow) but reframes it as mutual protection. Razorpay + invoice directly answers the unspoken "will you vanish with my advance?" fear — the #1 buyer worry. Never accept 100%-after; never take advance to a personal UPI.

> **If they push hard / high-ticket Ad 2 client:** offer a smaller proof-of-work first.
> ```
> Tell you what — let me build your homepage first. You see it live, then we do the 50/50 for the rest. Fair?
> ```

## 5. "Is it really 48 hours?"

> **Buyer:** "48 hours mein sach mein ho jayega? Sabhi log bolte hain fir 1 mahina lagta hai."

**Reply:**
```
Yes — and it's a real 48-hour clock, not a marketing line.

The clock starts the moment I have what I need from you: your logo (or I make a starter one), your text/photos, and your business details. From there, your site is live within 48 hours.

The only thing that slows it down is waiting on YOUR content — so if you send everything fast, you'll often have it sooner.

I'll give you a timestamp when we start, so you can hold me to it.
```

**Why this works:** honest about the *one* real dependency (client content) instead of over-promising. The "timestamp so you can hold me to it" is a confidence signal only someone who actually delivers would offer.

> **Guardrail reminder:** the 48-hour promise applies to the **₹2,000 Starter and ₹4,999 1-page** builds. For Ad 2's Pro / Commerce / Flagship tiers, never say 48h — say:
> ```
> The bigger builds (custom animation, online store, 500-page SEO) take a few days, not 48 hours — because they're built properly, not rushed. I'll give you a clear date upfront.
> ```

## 6. "Do I actually own the site?"

> **Buyer:** "After paying, is the website fully mine? Or do I have to keep paying you?"

**Reply:**
```
100% yours. This is where I'm different from the cheap shops.

✅ Domain — in your name
✅ Hosting — on YOUR own account (free for life, no renewal bill to me)
✅ The site, the code, all of it — yours to keep

There's no monthly lock-in. You can fire me tomorrow and your website keeps running exactly as it is.

The only thing you'd ever pay again is the domain renewal (~₹800–1,000/yr, paid directly to the registrar — at cost, not to me).
```

**Why this works:** directly kills the "hostage hosting" trap competitors rely on. Total-cost-of-ownership stated honestly (domain renewal at cost) so there's zero "hidden cost later" ambush. Ownership = the emotional close for a small-business owner who's been burned before.

## 7. "Any hidden charges? GST?"

> **Buyer:** "Is ₹2,000 the final price? GST extra toh nahi lagega?"

> ⚠️ **TAX / LEGAL NOTE — read before sending.** GST applicability depends on whether you are GST-registered (turnover threshold ₹20L for services in most states) and on how you invoice. The script below assumes **you are not yet charging GST and the price is all-inclusive.** The moment you register for GST, your pricing and these scripts must change to show GST separately. This is general guidance, **not** tax or legal advice — confirm your current obligation with a CA before relying on it.

**Reply (all-inclusive, pre-GST-registration):**
```
₹2,000 is the final price for the Starter build — that's it.

What's included, no extras: domain, hosting (free for life), starter logo + branding, the copy/text, and 1 free revision.

The only future cost is your domain renewal next year (~₹800–1,000, paid straight to the registrar at cost).

If you ever want extras later — an extra page (₹999), copywriting (₹499/page) — those are optional and I'll always tell you the price BEFORE doing anything. Nothing gets added to your bill without you saying yes.
```

**Why this works:** transparency *is* the category differentiator. Listing the add-on prices openly (instead of hiding them) signals you're not running the "page-tax ambush." The "nothing added without you saying yes" line directly answers the hidden-charge fear.

> **Once you ARE GST-registered**, switch to: `"Price is ₹2,000 + 18% GST = ₹2,360 all-in, with a proper GST invoice."` Never quietly absorb or hide GST once registered — show it as a line item.

## 8. "Will it rank #1 on Google?"

> **Buyer:** "Will my website come first on Google? Number 1 ranking guarantee hai?"

**Reply:**
```
I'll be straight with you, because anyone promising "guaranteed #1" is lying 🙂

Here's what I actually do and can promise:
✅ Built search-ready — clean structure Google understands
✅ Mobile-fast — speed is a real ranking factor
✅ Structured so AI assistants (ChatGPT, Gemini) can find and mention you too

What I can't promise: the exact rank — that's Google's call and depends on your competition. But you'll be properly findable, which is the part most cheap sites get wrong.

Rankings also build over time — want me to add the SEO pack so we push harder on that?
```

**Why this works:** honesty guardrail — never "guaranteed #1." Turns honesty into a *trust signal* vs competitors who over-promise. AEO line stays compliant ("structured so AI assistants can find you," not "guaranteed cited"). Soft upsell to the ₹9,999 programmatic-SEO pack at the end.

## 9. "I'll think about it" / "Too expensive" (Ad 2)

> **Buyer:** "Let me think about it." — or — "₹9,999 is a bit too much for me right now."

**Reply (to "I'll think about it"):**
```
Of course — take your time 🙂 One quick thing so your decision is easy:

Most "thinking" is really one of three questions:
1. Will it actually get me customers? → yes, it's built to convert, not just look nice
2. Can I trust him with the advance? → 50% only, Razorpay invoice, balance after it's live
3. Is the price fair? → you own everything for life, zero monthly lock-in

Tell me which one's on your mind and I'll clear it in one message. Or I can build a quick homepage sample so you're deciding on something real, not an idea.
```

**Reply (to "too expensive", Ad 2 — trade down, don't discount):**
```
Got it — let's fit it to your budget instead of cutting corners 🙂

If ₹9,999 is a stretch right now, the ₹4,999 Starter gets you a sharp, fast single-page site that does the main job: people find you, trust you, and contact you. You can always add pages later at ₹999 each as the business grows.

Same quality, same ownership, smaller start. Want me to set that one up?
```

**Why this works:** "I'll think about it" is almost always an *unspoken* objection — the script surfaces the real one (outcome / trust / price) so you can answer it. For "too expensive," you **trade down a tier, never discount** — discounting trains buyers to distrust your price and kills your margin. Trading down keeps the sale, the quality, and the dignity of the price.

> **Never do:** drop the price to "win" the deal. If pushed, remove scope (fewer pages), don't slash the rate. A discounted ₹2,000 build signals the ₹2,000 was inflated to begin with — which reopens objection #1.

## Golden rules (tape these above your desk)

1. **Lead with the renewal-trap wedge.** "Free for life on your own account" is your unbeatable line.
2. **A live demo link closes faster than any paragraph.** Keep one ready.
3. **Sell outcomes, never "cheap."** More customers, more trust, found on Google.
4. **Hold the 50%. Reframe it as buyer protection, not your demand.**
5. **Razorpay + invoice every time.** Never a personal UPI for an advance — it reads as a scam.
6. **Honesty is the strategy, not a constraint.** Every "we can't guarantee that" beats a competitor's lie.
7. **Trade down a tier before you discount. Never discount.**
8. **Never invent proof.** No fake testimonials, no "guaranteed #1," no "guaranteed cited by AI."
