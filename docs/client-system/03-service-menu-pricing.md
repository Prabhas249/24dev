# PART A — INTERNAL MASTER (founder only — never send)

> This part exists so you quote consistently and protect margin. It exposes cost math, capacity, and exclusions. **The lead never sees Part A.**

## A0. How to use this internally

- **Anchor first in your head, middle in your mouth.** When a lead is unsure, mention the Flagship exists ("we go up to ₹49,999 for fully automated 500-page sites") then land them on **₹9,999 Business**. The high number does the selling; you do the steering.
- **One badge.** Exactly one tier is "Most popular" per menu (Ad 1 → the ₹2,000; Ad 2 → the ₹9,999). Don't badge two — it kills the decoy effect.
- **Charm pricing stays** on low/mid (₹1,999 / ₹4,999 / ₹9,999 / ₹14,999). It tests higher-converting for value buyers. Keep it.
- **Payments:** 50% advance to start, 50% on delivery. Razorpay link. No work begins before advance clears.
- **Never expose:** hours budget, cost, margin, capacity caps, or the word "from" rationale. Those live only here.

## A1. Capacity caps (protect delivery — the real bottleneck)

| Tier | Max active in parallel | Why |
|---|---|---|
| ₹1,999 / ₹2,000 Starter | up to 3/day **only if** you have a helper for delivery | 24–48h promise breaks if you stack solo |
| ₹4,999 Starter (Ad 2) | 2/day | 1-page, fast, but copy + design polish eats time |
| ₹9,999 Business | 1–2 active | 5–7 pages + copywriting is the real work block |
| ₹14,999 Pro | 1 active | custom animation needs focus |
| ₹24,999 Commerce | 1 active | store + payments testing |
| ₹49,999 Flagship | 1 at a time, hard stop | 500 pages + headless = multi-day, don't double-book |

> **Rule:** when you have 2 straight months of ₹80k+ profit, hire delivery help before you raise volume. Delivery is the bottleneck, not leads.

## A2. The ₹2,000 Starter (Ad 1) — internal scope

- **Outcome sold:** "a proper website live in under 48 hours, that you fully own."
- **Included:** 1-page site (long-scroll, mobile-first), domain setup + **year 1 paid**, deploy to **client's own** hosting account (free for life there), **starter** logo + brand colours, all copy written for them, WhatsApp/call button, Google Map embed, basic on-page SEO (title/description/headings), 1 free revision.
- **NOT included (scope guard — do not give away):** custom logo beyond starter mark, more than 1 page (→ ₹999/extra page), e-commerce, bookings/forms beyond basic, blog/CMS, ongoing edits after the 1 revision (→ Care plan), stock-photo licensing beyond free sources, content in >1 language.
- **Hours budget:** 3–5h end-to-end. If a lead's needs push past that (multi-page, store, lots of custom copy), **stop and upsell** to ₹4,999/₹9,999 — don't absorb it at ₹2,000.
- **True cost:** domain ~₹0–₹900 (year 1, varies by TLD) + your time. Hosting = ₹0 (their account). **Margin floor: keep gross ≥ 70% after domain.** If a `.com` is pricey, still deliver year 1; renewal is theirs at cost.
- **Upsell triggers:** they ask for a 2nd page → ₹999/page or jump to ₹9,999. They want a real logo → ₹7,999 brand kit. They'll need monthly edits → Care ₹1,000/mo. They say "and I sell products" → ₹24,999 Commerce.

## A3. Ad 2 tiers — internal scope

### ₹4,999 Starter (Ad 2) — "sharp 1-page"
- **Outcome:** "one sharp page that makes a serious business look credible and drives enquiries."
- **Included:** 1 high-craft page, custom section layout, copywriting for that page, contact/enquiry form, on-page SEO, mobile-perfect, 1 free revision, ownership + deploy to their account.
- **NOT included:** multi-page, blog/CMS, custom animation, store. Those are the tiers above.
- **Hours:** 5–8h. **Margin floor ≥ 70%.**
- **Upsell trigger:** "can I add pages / a blog?" → that's the ₹9,999 Business (don't bolt 4 pages onto a ₹4,999).

### ₹9,999 Business — ⭐ PUSH THIS (the decoy target / compromise tier)
- **Outcome:** "a complete 5–7 page website that turns visitors into enquiries — and that you can update yourself."
- **Included:** 5–7 pages, **professional copywriting across the site**, **blog/CMS you can post to yourself**, enquiry forms, on-page SEO across all pages, mobile-perfect, basic analytics, 1 free revision, full ownership + deploy to their account.
- **NOT included:** custom scroll/animation (→ ₹14,999 Pro), online store (→ ₹24,999), programmatic SEO at scale (→ ₹9,999 add-on or ₹49,999 tier).
- **Hours:** 12–18h. **Margin floor ≥ 65%** (copywriting is the cost; protect it). **This is your bread-and-butter MRR feeder** — every Business client is a Care ₹1,999/mo candidate.
- **Why you push it:** it's the centre option between ₹4,999 and ₹14,999. Buyers anchor away from cheapest and dearest → they land here. Badge it "Most popular."

### ₹14,999 Pro — "conversion design + custom animation"
- **Outcome:** "a website that feels premium and is built to convert — custom motion, conversion-focused layout."
- **Included:** everything in Business **plus** custom animation/scroll interactions, conversion-focused design pass, deeper copy, priority delivery.
- **NOT included:** store/payments (₹24,999), 500-page auto-SEO (₹49,999).
- **Hours:** 18–28h. **Margin floor ≥ 60%.**
- **Upsell trigger:** "I want to sell online" → ₹24,999 Commerce.

### ₹24,999 Commerce — "online store OR 3D hero"
- **Outcome:** "start selling online — product catalogue + Razorpay checkout — **or** a standout 3D hero that nobody else in your market has."
- **Included:** EITHER (a) online store: product pages, cart, Razorpay checkout, order flow; OR (b) premium 3D/interactive hero experience on a marketing site. Copy + SEO + mobile-perfect + ownership included. Client picks one path.
- **NOT included:** unlimited SKUs (agree a catalogue size up front), warehouse/inventory integrations, multilingual, 500-page SEO.
- **Hours:** 25–40h. **Margin floor ≥ 55%.** Scope the SKU count before quoting — open-ended catalogues kill margin.
- **Upsell trigger:** "I need this in Hindi + Telugu too" or "I want hundreds of location/keyword pages" → ₹49,999 Flagship.

### ₹49,999 Flagship — ⚓ THE ANCHOR (rarely sold, always quoted)
- **Outcome:** "a serious growth engine — a fast headless site with up to 500 auto-generated SEO pages and multilingual reach, built to be found at scale."
- **Included:** headless architecture, **up to 500 programmatic/auto-SEO pages** ("from"/MVP scope), multilingual, the works. **This is the number you mention to make ₹9,999–₹14,999 feel easy.**
- **HONESTY GUARD:** always frame as **"from ₹49,999" / MVP scope**. 500 pages = the ceiling of the package, defined by a template + data feed you agree up front — not 500 hand-written pages. SEO = search-ready structure; **rankings depend on Google, never promise #1.**
- **NOT included by default:** ongoing content ops, paid ad management, anything outside the agreed template/data scope.
- **Hours:** multi-day, 40h+. **Quote bespoke** — treat ₹49,999 as the floor, scope up from there. **Take 50% advance, no exceptions.**
- **Why it exists:** decoy anchor. Even if you sell one a quarter, its job on the menu is to make the middle look reasonable.

## A5. Terms (internal — confirm with CA/lawyer; see banner)

- **Payment:** 50% advance (work starts on clearance) + 50% on delivery, before final handover/transfer.
- **Revisions:** 1 free per tier. Further rounds billed (set your rate; suggest ₹499–₹999/round for Starter, more for Pro+). State this only if asked.
- **Ownership:** client owns the final site, domain, and content. Transfer on full payment.
- **Hosting/domain:** deployed to the **client's own** account; domain year-1 included; **renewals are the client's, at cost.** Say this plainly.
- **Refunds:** advance covers work begun. Define your policy (e.g. pre-work-start = refundable minus gateway fee; post-start = pro-rated/none). **Get this in writing and CA-reviewed.**
- **GST:** if/when registered, prices are **+18% GST** unless you state "inclusive." Decide and label consistently on every quote and invoice. **Confirm with CA.**
- **Timelines:** Starter/Business 24–48h (Ad 1) / a couple of days (Ad 2 lower tiers). **Pro/Commerce/Flagship take several days — say so up front; never promise 24–48h on premium tiers.**
