# 24dev — WhatsApp Sales Script Pack

**Paste-ready quick replies for both Meta ads. Save each block as a WhatsApp "Quick Reply" so you can fire it in seconds.**

These scripts are tuned to the three things that actually move the needle on WhatsApp:

1. **Speed** — your first reply must fire in **seconds**, not minutes. A lead answered within 5 minutes is ~9× more likely to convert. Outside that, you bleed the deal.
2. **The 72-hour free window** — because both ads are Click-to-WhatsApp, every lead opens a **72-hour free-messaging window** the moment they message (as long as you reply within 24h). Do the **entire sell-and-close inside those first 72 hours**.
3. **Spacing, not spamming** — the full follow-up is **3 touches over ~11 days**, each a *different angle*. **Never send 5 messages in 24 hours** — that's the fastest route to a block/report and account restriction.

> **How to use this file:** Each script is labelled **WHEN to send it**. Replace anything in `{curly braces}` with the real detail. Keep the Razorpay link IN the chat — never push a lead to a separate site or a call (in-context payment lifts checkout completion 15–25%).

---

## SECTION 0 — The instant-acknowledge (fire in SECONDS, before anything else)

**WHEN:** The literal first second a lead messages, if you can't immediately type a full quote (you're driving, in a build, asleep-but-awake). This buys you time without losing the speed advantage. If you CAN reply fully right away, skip this and go straight to Section 1.

```
Hey! 👋 This is the 24dev founder — got your message. Give me ~2 minutes and I'll send you everything (price, what's included, timeline). 🙌
```

**Then deliver the real reply within those 2 minutes.** Never promise "a few minutes" and vanish — that breaks the one promise the whole brand is built on.

## SECTION 1 — Instant welcome reply (one version per ad)

The structure that converts, used in both:
- Warm 1-line greeting + **mirror exactly what they asked for** (their pre-filled text → instant relevance)
- **One outcome line**, not a feature list
- **Price as a bundle** (domain + hosting + logo + copy) so it reads as value, not cost
- **One clear next step / single question** — keeps the 24h window open, avoids choice paralysis

## SECTION 6 — 3-touch follow-up for non-responders

**The rule that protects your account:** 3 touches over ~11 days, each a **different angle** — never a repeat of "just checking in." All three sit inside or just past the free window. **Never stack messages; never 5 in 24h.** If they reply at any point, drop the sequence and go back to Section 2/3.

---

### Touch 1 — SAME DAY (a few hours after no reply, still Day 1)

**WHEN:** They opened the chat / you sent a welcome or quote, then went quiet for ~3–6 hours on the same day. Light, helpful, low-pressure — one specific question.

```
No rush at all, {Name} 😊 — just so I can keep your spot, what's the name of your business? I can have a first look ready for you fast once I know.

**Ad 2:**
```
