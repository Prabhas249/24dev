# 1. Refund, Cancellation & Guarantee Policy

We want you happy with your website — and we want you to know **exactly** when money can come back and when it can't, before you pay. No surprises. No fine-print traps.

## How payment works
- **50% advance** to start your project, **50% on delivery** before the final site goes live on your account.
- All payments are collected securely through **Razorpay**.
- Prices shown on the tier page are the **total** price for that scope. Add-ons (extra pages, logo/brand kit, copywriting, SEO pack, Website Care) are priced separately and clearly listed before you pay.

## Our delivery promise
- **Rs 2,000 Starter** and **Rs 4,999 Starter (1-page):** delivered in **under 48 hours** *from the moment you hand over your content and access* (see "When the clock starts" below).
- **Rs 9,999 Business and above (Pro / Commerce / Flagship):** these are bigger builds and take **a few working days** — we agree a written delivery date with you on WhatsApp before you pay. We do **not** promise 48 hours on these tiers.

## ✅ When your advance IS refundable (full refund)
You get your **full 50% advance back** in any of these cases:

1. **We miss the agreed delivery window** and the delay is our fault (not waiting on you). Under the Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules 2020, we cannot keep your money if we deliver late or deliver something that doesn't match what was advertised — and we won't try to.
2. **You reject the first design concept**, in writing, **before we begin development.** We'll show you an initial design direction; if it's not for you and you tell us before we start building the actual site, you can cancel and we refund the advance in full.
3. **We can't deliver the scope we sold you** — if for any reason we cannot build what your tier promised, you get a full refund.
4. **The delivered website is genuinely deficient** — it doesn't work, doesn't match the agreed scope, or doesn't match what the ad/tier page advertised. We'll fix it first (see your free revision); if we still can't deliver what was promised, you're entitled to a refund of the affected amount.

## ❌ When your advance is NOT refundable
To be fair to a one-person studio that starts real work the moment you book, the advance is **non-refundable** in these cases:

1. **You go quiet / don't send content.** If you stop responding or don't hand over the content, logos, text, or access we need to build, and the project stalls **on your side**, the advance covers the time and slots we held for you.
2. **You change your mind after development has started** (and you'd already approved the first design direction).
3. **You cancel for reasons unrelated to our performance** — e.g. you decided not to have a website, found someone else, or business plans changed — once we've begun building.

## ❌ When NO refund applies (project complete)
Once the website is **built and delivered** — meaning it's deployed to your own hosting account and handed over, or you've approved it for go-live — the project is complete and **payments are non-refundable.** At that point you own a finished website. Your **1 free revision** (below) is how we make a delivered site right, not a refund.

## Your 1 free revision
Every project includes **one free round of revisions** after the first build. Tell us what to change (within the agreed scope) and we'll do it at no cost. Additional revision rounds, or changes that expand the original scope (new pages, new features), are quoted as paid add-ons.

## "Guarantee" — what we honestly mean
- We **do not** offer a blanket "100% money-back, no questions asked" guarantee, because that's not honest for custom work that we build to order. What we *do* guarantee is the conditional refund rights above — and we'll never hide the conditions.
- We **never** guarantee Google rankings (#1, page-one, or otherwise). We build your site to be **search-ready**; where you actually rank depends on Google's algorithm, your competition, and time.
- We **never** guarantee that AI assistants (ChatGPT, Gemini, etc.) will cite or recommend you. We structure your site so AI assistants **can** find and read it; we can't promise any AI will quote you.
- "Free domain" = we register and set it up, **year 1 included**; **renewals from year 2 are at cost, paid by you.**
- "Free hosting for life" = your site is deployed to **your own hosting account**, so there's nothing for us to renew and nothing extra to pay us. You control it forever.
- "Free logo" = a **starter brand kit** (logo + basic brand colours/fonts), not unlimited custom logo design. A full custom logo + brand kit is the Rs 7,999 add-on.
