# 07 — Retention, Testimonials & Referral Pack

**The moment the site goes live is the most valuable moment you'll ever have with a client.** They're excited, grateful, and looking at their new website for the first time. That excitement decays *fast* — within 48 hours it's gone. This pack is built to harvest that peak: get the proof your ads need, lock in recurring Care revenue, and turn one happy client into the next two.

Everything below is paste-ready. Copy the plain-text blocks straight into WhatsApp. Swap `[brackets]` for real details.

---

## The 4-Step Handoff Sequence (the whole flow at a glance)

| When | What you send | Goal |
|---|---|---|
| **At delivery** | Final-payment + handoff message (A) | Collect 50%, deliver, hit the emotional peak |
| **Same message, right after they confirm** | Testimonial + screenshot-permission ask (B1) | Capture the praise while it's fresh |
| **Same message / next reply** | Google review one-tap ask (B2) | Build public proof for the ads |
| **Same message** | Care plan pitch (C) | Lock recurring revenue at the peak |
| **Same message** | Referral ask (D) | Turn 1 client into the next 2 |
| **Day 3–4 (free-revision check-in)** | ONE gentle reminder for review + referral | The single allowed nudge |

**Golden rule: ask ONCE at handoff, nudge ONCE on day 3–4. Never more.** A third ask costs you the goodwill.

## A. Final-Payment + Delivery Handoff Message

```

Bas last step — baaki ka 50%:

💳 Pay link: [Razorpay link]
```

## B. Testimonial & Review Scripts

> **Why this matters more than anything else here:** your ads are *not allowed* to use fake testimonials (honesty guardrail). Real proof is the only proof you get. Every happy client is a chance to bank one line of real social proof + one Google review. Ask while the praise is fresh — within 24–48 hours of delivery, ideally in the same thread as the "it's LIVE" message.

### B1 — Capture the 1-line result + permission to screenshot

**Trigger:** they reply to A3 with anything positive ("wow", "love it", "perfect", a 🙏 or 😍). That unprompted praise IS your testimonial — you just need consent to use it.

```
That genuinely means a lot, [Name]. 🙏

Quick favour — would it be okay if I featured your words on the 24dev website and Instagram? Just a screenshot of your message, with your name + business. Helps other small businesses trust that this is real.

Totally fine if you'd rather not — no pressure at all. 🙂
```

> **When they say yes** → that "yes" in the chat IS your proof of consent. Keep the thread. Screenshot only the real message. Never edit their words. Never fabricate.

**Optional sharper version — ask for one specific line:**

```
Would you mind dropping me one line on what you liked most — could be the speed, the design, or just how easy it was? I'd love to share it (with your name + business, with your permission) so other shop owners know they're in good hands. 🙏
```

> A line like *"Got my whole shop online in a day for ₹2,000 — looks better than sites costing 10x"* is gold. That's the kind of outcome-led proof your ads need.

### B2 — Google Business review (one tap, zero friction)

> **Friction kills reviews.** Never say "search for us on Google." Send the *direct review link* so it's literally one tap. Set up your Google Business Profile review short-link once, then reuse it forever.

```
One last thing and I'll leave you to enjoy the new site 😄

If it's not too much trouble, a quick Google review would genuinely help 24dev reach more business owners like you. It's one tap:

⭐ [direct Google review link]

Even one line about the experience makes a huge difference. Thank you, [Name]! 🙏
```

**Shop / Hinglish version:**

```
Ek aur chhoti si request 🙏

Agar 2 minute ho toh Google pe ek review de do — isse mujhe aur business owners tak pahunchne mein madad milti hai. Bas ek tap:

⭐ [direct Google review link]

Ek line bhi kaafi hai. Bahut bahut shukriya! 😊
```

> **How to get your one-tap review link (do this once):**
> Google Business Profile → "Ask for reviews" / "Get more reviews" → copy the short link (looks like `g.page/r/...`). Save it. Paste it into every B2 ask from now on.

### B3 — The single allowed reminder (Day 3–4 only)

> Fold this into the free-revision check-in so it never feels like nagging. This is your ONE reminder — don't send a second.

```
Hey [Name] — checking in! How's the new site treating you? Anything you'd like tweaked? (Your free revision is still on the table 🙂)

And if you got a sec — that quick Google review would mean a lot:
⭐ [direct Google review link]
```

## C. Care Plan Upsell — "Your Site's Fuel"

> **Pitch it at handoff, riding the peak.** This is your recurring-revenue engine. ₹1,000/mo (Starter sites) or ₹1,999/mo (Business+ sites). The frame is *insurance + fuel*, NOT "maintenance." Sell peace of mind, not chores.

**The mindset (don't paste this — internalise it):**
- A car needs fuel and oil changes to keep running. A website is the same — it needs updates, security, backups. Sell that, not "support tickets."
- Lead with the **3 S's: Speed, Security, Savings.** *Fixing a hacked or broken site costs thousands; ₹1,000/mo prevents the disaster.*
- Frame as a **prerequisite, not an add-on**: *"so you focus on your business, not the tech."*

### C1 — Business+ version (₹1,999/mo)

```
Your site is live and 100% yours — but here's the honest bit, [Name]:

A website is like a car. Buy it, then keep fuel in it. Websites need updates, security patches, backups, and the occasional fix — otherwise they slow down, break, or get attacked. Fixing a hacked or down site later costs thousands and days of lost customers.

That's what Website Care covers — your site's fuel:

🛡️ Security + monitoring (we catch issues before they cost you)
💾 Automatic backups (one bad update can't wipe you out)
⚡ Speed + uptime checks (a slow site loses customers)
🔧 Small edits + fixes handled for you (you stay focused on business)

₹1,999/month. Cancel anytime. Think of it as insurance that's cheaper than one emergency.

Want me to switch it on? It starts today, no setup needed.
```

### C2 — Starter / shop version (₹1,000/mo — Hinglish OK)

```
Website live hai, poori tarah aapki [Name]. 🎉 Ek choti si baat —

Website gaadi jaisi hai: ek baar le li, ab thoda fuel chahiye chalti rehne ke liye. Updates, backup, security, chhoti-moti fixes — warna site slow ho jaati hai ya band pad jaati hai. Baad mein theek karwana mehenga padta hai.

Website Care isi ke liye hai — aapki site ka fuel:

🛡️ Security + monitoring
💾 Auto backup (kuch galat hua toh wapas la denge)
⚡ Speed check (slow site = customer chala jaata hai)
🔧 Chhoti edits/fixes main sambhaal lunga

Sirf ₹1,000/mahina. Kabhi bhi band kar sakte ho. Ek baar ki emergency se sasta.

Chालू kar dun? Aaj se shuru, koi extra setup nahi.
```

### C3 — Soft close (if they go quiet on Care)

> Don't push. Plant it and let it sit. One line, no pressure.

```
No rush on Care at all — the site runs fine as-is. Just know it's there as a safety net whenever you want it. I've got your back either way. 🙂
```

## D. Referral Ask + Light Incentive

> **Make it two-sided** — reward the referrer AND the friend. People share to *give their friend something*, not mainly to earn. Build the ask into the handoff, the invoice, and the Care renewal — not one big blast.

### The 24dev referral offer (simple, fixed-value — pick what fits your margins)

- **Their friend gets:** ₹500 off their build (or a free extra page).
- **The referrer gets:** ₹500 credit toward their next project / add-on — OR one free month of Website Care.
- **Repeat-referrer bonus (optional ladder):** refer 3 → a free *quarter* of Care. Keeps your best advocates sending.

> Keep it legible. One clear number beats a complicated points scheme for a solo op.

### D1 — Referral ask at handoff

```
Last thing, [Name] — and this is a win-win 🙂

If you know another business owner who needs a website, send them my way. Here's the deal:

🎁 They get ₹500 off their site
🎁 You get ₹500 credit (or a free month of Website Care) once theirs goes live

Just tell them to message me and mention your name. That simple. Most of my work comes from people like you spreading the word — and I make it worth your while. 🙏
```

**Shop / Hinglish version:**

```
Ek aakhri baat — ye dono ke liye faayda hai 😄

Koi aur dukaan/business wale ko website chahiye? Mujhe bhej do:

🎁 Unko ₹500 off
🎁 Aapko ₹500 credit (ya ek mahina free Website Care) jab unki site live ho jaaye

Bas unse bolna mera naam mention karein. Itna hi. Aapka thoda sa support, mera bahut bada sahaara. 🙏
```

### D2 — Referral nudge at Care renewal / later touchpoint

```
Hope the site's still bringing you customers, [Name]! 🙌 Quick reminder — if you ever send a fellow business owner my way, you both get ₹500 (and you can stack it toward your Care plan). The offer's always open.
```

## Quick Reference — the order you actually send things

At a successful delivery, your messages go out in this sequence (often back-to-back in one sitting):

1. **A1/A2** → balance pay link
2. *(payment clears)*
3. **A3** → "You're LIVE" + full handover
4. **B1** → permission to screenshot their praise *(after they react positively)*
5. **B2** → one-tap Google review link
6. **C1/C2** → Care plan "fuel" pitch
7. **D1** → referral offer
8. *(Day 3–4)* **B3** → check-in + single review reminder

> Don't dump all of these in one giant wall of text. Send A3 first, let them react, *then* layer in B → C → D conversationally. The praise reply in B1 is your cue that the moment is hot.
