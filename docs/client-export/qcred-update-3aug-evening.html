One setting on your side, and five questions

Why your RM couldn’t see Partners — two causes, both now resolved

The crash was our defect, fixed as above. But there was a second layer: the RM role’s partners — view permission had been switched off in Settings → Roles during yesterday’s exploration of the permission screens. That’s easily done while trying things out — the screen applies changes immediately. We have restored the RM role to its original permission set, so your RM logins now see Partners again.

Worth knowing: every permission change is recorded in the audit trail with who and when, so this is always reconstructable. If you’d like, we can add a confirmation step before role permissions change — say the word.

Questions we need answered — so nothing is built on a guess

Each of these changes real behaviour. One-line answers are enough.

1 · Which roles should see team-only data?

The machinery is live. Today: RM and Maker see own records, managerial roles see all. Tell us which roles should switch to team (own + everyone reporting to them) and we’ll set them — or you can, in Settings → Roles, once you confirm the intent.

2 · Who counts as “corporate office” for employee approvals?

We’ve granted approval rights to Checker, National Business Head and Super Admin. Should anyone else hold it — Divisional Managers, for instance?

3 · Region for the lender catalogue — states, or zones?

The lender funnel currently narrows by state (Maharashtra, Karnataka…). If your team thinks in zones (North / South / West / East), we’ll switch the first step. With 100+ lenders coming this choice decides how useful the funnel is day-to-day.

4 · Bonus slabs — best slab, or stacking?

Target bonuses currently apply as best-slab-wins (reach ₹1 Cr, the whole month earns the higher rate). If any of your lenders’ schemes accumulate tier by tier instead, tell us which — it changes payout arithmetic and must be right before real money runs.

5 · Partner share and TDS percentages

Auto-calculated payouts currently use 60% partner share and 5% TDS as placeholders. Please confirm your real figures — including whether share differs by tier (Sub-DSA vs Connector).
