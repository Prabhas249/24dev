Built to keep you free of lock-in

Integration rails, ready to switch on

No third-party sign-in dependency

Login is built into your own system rather than rented from a vendor. There is no per-user fee as your partner network grows toward 5,000+, and nothing to migrate later.

Segregation of duties — why Maker and Checker exist

A Maker can raise a payout but never approve it. A Checker can approve and mark it paid but cannot raise one, and cannot see leads outside a read-only view. The same split applies to partner activation. This means no single staff member can move money from creation to payment on their own — the control auditors and lenders expect from a distribution business.

Why calculated, not uploaded

The reference CRM you shared required a commission PDF to be uploaded per payout. That makes every figure a manual entry and every dispute unresolvable. Here the arithmetic is derived from the disbursed amount and the product rate, shown on screen, and recorded in the audit trail — so when a partner questions a payout six months later, you can show exactly how it was reached.

How documents are protected

KYC files are identity documents, so they are held in private storage — not on a public web address. A file can only be opened through the application, which checks who is asking and whether that person is allowed to see that specific document. We verified this on the live system: a signed-in authorised user receives the file; a visitor who is not signed in is refused. Only JPG, PNG, WEBP and PDF are accepted, up to 5 MB.

Guide 1 — Approving and activating a partner

Open Partners and select the partner marked Pending.
Check the Documents tab. Verify each document, or reject it with a reason so the partner can replace it.
Record training. Until this is done, activation is blocked — this is your framework §4.
Approve, then activate. The partner code is issued automatically in the correct format for their tier and city.
Tell them. They can now sign in and submit business immediately.

Guide 2 — Working a lead to disbursement

Open the lead from Leads, or from any number on the dashboard.
Assign it to the relationship manager who will own it.
Verify documents. If something is missing, move the stage to Docs Query — the partner sees it and can respond.
Apply to a lender using Apply More. The link carries your DSA code so the lender attributes the business to QCred.
Update the stage as the lender progresses. Add remarks; mark them internal if the partner should not see them.
Record the disbursement with the final amount. Commission is calculated at this point.

Guide 3 — Paying a partner

Maker: open Payouts and raise the payout against the disbursed lead. The amount is pre-calculated.
Checker: review and approve. The system will not let the same person do both.
Make the transfer in your bank, then mark it paid and record the reference number.
The partner sees it in their Earnings screen with the reference — fewer “where is my payout” calls.
