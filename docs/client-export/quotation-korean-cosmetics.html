01Our understanding of your model

Your customers browse a polished, beauty-brand-grade catalogue on the website; when they tap Order on WhatsApp, their full cart (products, quantities, total — and the influencer's reference code, when applicable) arrives pre-filled in your WhatsApp chat, where you confirm and close the sale. Every price below is scoped to exactly this flow. No payment gateway is included or needed.

06A transparency note on location tracking

Precise GPS location of a visitor requires a browser consent popup — a browser and legal requirement no agency can bypass. Our default implementation captures city-level location silently via IP on every landing visit (compliant, no popups); consent-based precise location can be added as an option.
