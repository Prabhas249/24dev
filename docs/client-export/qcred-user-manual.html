Part 1

Getting started

Signing in

Open the platform address and sign in with your mobile number and password. Staff land on the CRM dashboard; partners land on the partner portal. The same login page serves both.

If you’re new — joining by invitation
  1. You receive a private, single-use link (valid 14 days).
  2. Open it, check your details, and set your own password (minimum 8 characters).
  3. Employees: your account then waits for approval — your supervisor or the corporate office confirms you, and you’ll be able to sign in the moment they do.
  4. Partners: you can sign in straight away and complete your profile, but lead submission unlocks only once QCred approves and activates your partner code.
Changing your password
  1. Profile (bottom of the menu) → Password → enter current and new → Update password.
Every button tells you what’s happening When you save anything, the button shows “Saving…” with a spinner, then flashes “Updated ✓”. While saving, it locks — so double-clicking can never create a duplicate.
If you see “You don’t have access” Nothing is broken. Your role simply doesn’t include that permission — the page names the exact permission to ask your administrator for (Settings → Roles & permissions).

The to-do queue

The first thing on your dashboard is Needs your attention — built from what your role can actually action: approvals waiting on you, documents to verify, follow-ups due, leads unassigned. Empty queue = genuinely nothing waiting. Every card is clickable.

Part 2

The CRM, section by section

Dashboard all staff

Your day at a glance: the to-do queue, KPI cards (leads, disbursed value, partners, pending payouts), the pipeline by stage, follow-ups due, and product/partner breakdowns. Every number is clickable and opens the filtered list behind it. Cards you don’t have permission for simply don’t appear.

Leads per role scope

The heart of the CRM. What you see here follows your data scope — your own leads, your team’s, or all (Part 4). Search by name, mobile or lead number; filter by status and product; export to CSV. Phone numbers are click-to-call and WhatsApp everywhere.

Create a lead
  1. Leads → + New lead. Pick the product — the form changes with it.
  2. Enter customer details. PAN, Aadhaar and mobile are validated as you submit — Aadhaar against its official checksum, so a typo is caught here, not by the lender.
  3. Submit. The lead gets its code (e.g. QC-L-00042) and enters the pipeline.
Work a lead to disbursement
  1. Open the lead → Assign it to the RM who’ll own it.
  2. Verify documents; if something is missing, set stage to Docs Query — the partner sees it with your remark and can respond.
  3. Log in with lender to apply — the link carries QCred’s DSA code and the lead reference, so attribution is automatic. One lead can hold applications to several lenders, each tracked separately.
  4. Update the stage as the lender progresses. Remarks marked internal are never shown to the partner — enforced in the database, not just hidden.
  5. Schedule follow-ups; overdue ones surface on your dashboard.
The 18 pipeline stages Pending · Allocated · Submitted · Under-Process · Docs Query-EA · Docs Query-LA · Login · Credit Review · Approved · Sanctioned · Agreement · Disbursed · Part-Disbursed · On Hold · Issue Raised · Issue Resolved · Rejected · Dropped — each with its own colour, all editable in Settings without a developer.

Partners partners.view

The channel network. Opens with the Access levels grid (what each partner login can do, plus the four field tiers with live counts), then the list with codes, tiers, lead counts and status filters. Every partner file has seven tabs: Overview, Basic, Contact, Bank, Documents, Leads, Reviews.

Approve & activate a new partner
  1. Open the partner (they arrive as Pending).
  2. Documents tab → verify each, or reject with a reason (they can re-upload).
  3. Overview → Mark training complete. Until this is recorded, the Activate button does not exist — training before activation is mandatory (framework §4).
  4. Approve, then Activate. Their code is issued automatically in the correct format for their tier and geography (e.g. QC-MH-MUM-SDA-0001) — permanent, never reused.
Commercial terms — TDS per partner On each partner’s Overview: agreement type and TDS. Commission agreement → 2% · Retainer agreement → 10% (auto-filled, overridable — it follows tax law, so it’s editable data, not code). Share % too. The payout calculation uses these.

The money screens

Disbursements disbursements.view

Where a sanctioned case is recorded as money-out-the-door — and where the payout is born.

Record a disbursement
  1. Disbursements → find the sanctioned case.
  2. Enter disbursed amount, lender, and the lender programme — the programme decides the commission rate, so pick the one the file was actually booked under.
  3. Enter the commercials: fees collected, interest rate, property value, tenure. LTV is calculated automatically from amount ÷ property value.
  4. Record. The lead moves to Disbursed and the partner’s payout is raised automatically — calculated, not typed.

Payouts view: most · release: Checker + Super Admin ONLY

Commission tracked as two separate legs: what the lender owes QCred (base rate + any target bonus from the programme), and what QCred owes the partner (their share, minus their TDS). So you always know whether you’re waiting to be paid or waiting to pay.

The payout lifecycle — maker-checker enforced
  1. Payout is raised (automatically on disbursement, or by a Maker).
  2. A Checker reviews and approves — the system physically refuses to let whoever raised it approve it.
  3. The transfer is made in your bank — the CRM never moves money itself.
  4. Mark paid with the UTR and mode. The partner is notified and sees it in Earnings.
Who can release money By QCred’s own rule: payout approval and payment are held by Checker and Super Admin only. Managers and heads can see and raise, never release.

Products products.view / edit

The 9 loan products partners submit against. Everything on the card is editable in place — name, short code, kind, category, description — and the list is reorderable (that order is what partners see). Turning a product off hides it from the portal without touching existing leads. Each product drives its own application form.

Lenders & Best Match

The lender catalogue all staff · partners (no payout)

Built for 100+ lenders, it narrows in QCred’s hierarchy — Zone → Region → Product — with a live count on every chip, ending in a table of matching programmes: eligibility (FOIR, LTV, CIBIL, property and profile types), commercials (rates, ticket sizes, tenure) and QCred’s payout %. Partners see the same catalogue without the payout column — it is excluded from the data, not hidden on screen.

Grids, where a lender publishes them Most lenders quote one LTV and one maximum — the screen asks for one number. Where a lender publishes a grid (Poonawalla: LTV per property type, maximum per city tier), tick the grid option and fill only the cells they publish; blanks fall back to the single figure.
Add a lender and its programmes (Manage lenders)
  1. Lenders → Manage lenders → Add lender (name, sector).
  2. + Add programme per programme the lender publishes — one row each, like Poonawalla’s eleven.
  3. Fill eligibility, commercials, and Payout — the base % QCred earns, plus target bonus slabs (“₹1 Cr in the month → +0.25%”; the highest slab reached applies).
  4. Or fill the Excel template and send it to 24dev — it loads directly, exactly as Poonawalla’s sheet was loaded.

Best Match all staff

Describe the case — product, amount, property value and type, profile, CIBIL, city tier — and every active programme is scored and ranked. The ranking explains itself: each match lists its reasons (“✓ LTV 60% within 80%”) and each rejection its blockers (“✕ ceiling 55% for industrial property”). Ranked by eligibility first, payout second. As real volume flows, per-lender approval and disbursement rates feed the same score — the learning half switches on with data.

Sales contacts

Each lender carries its sales directory — NSM, zonal and regional managers with phone and email (Poonawalla’s 23 are loaded). When a file is stuck, the person to chase is one tap away, under the lender in Manage view.

Marketing marketing.view / upload

A tiled gallery with image previews. Upload files directly (JPG/PNG/WEBP/PDF, ≤5 MB) or paste a link; publish, archive, restore. Every download is recorded — who took what, when, with their partner code — so you can see which creatives the network actually uses.

Reports reports.view

Lead, disbursement, partner-performance and payout reports — filter by date, product, state, partner, status; export any of them to CSV for Excel. Exports respect your data scope.

People and configuration

Employees employees.view

Opens with the role cards (each role, its permission count, how far it sees), then the staff list with role, Reports to, status and last login.

Add an employee — two ways
  1. Invite by link (recommended): name, mobile, role → Create invite → send them the link. They set their own password — nothing to share over WhatsApp.
  2. Add directly with a temporary password, for someone standing next to you.
  3. Either way, set Reports to — this is the reporting line that team visibility and approvals walk.
Approve a new employee
  1. New employees show awaiting approval and cannot sign in yet.
  2. Anyone in their reporting line above them — however many layers up — sees the Approve button, as does the corporate office (Checker, Super Admin). It also lands in the approver’s dashboard to-do.
  3. Approve → they can sign in immediately.

Announcements announcements.view / create

Publish to the whole network or a chosen tier. Partners see them on their dashboard and notifications screen.

Settings settings.view / edit — admin

PanelWhat you control there
Roles & permissionsEvery role’s permissions (52 switches across 14 modules) and its data access — Own / Team / All. Changes apply immediately and are audit-logged.
Pipeline stagesRename, recolour, reorder, mark final, delete stages and sub-stages in place. Deleting a stage that leads sit on is refused — records are never orphaned.
NumberingLead, partner and application code formats, with a live preview. Partner codes follow the coding framework (state, city, tier, per-combination sequence); lead codes can carry state and product segments.
Company detailsName, support contacts and similar text used across the platform.
A caution on Roles Permission changes take effect the moment you save — unticking view on a module removes that screen for everyone holding the role. Every change is recorded in the audit trail with who and when, so anything can be reconstructed and reversed.

The Partner Portal

What your channel partners see when they sign in. Same platform, scoped hard: a partner sees their own business and their team’s — nothing else is ever sent to their browser.

ScreenWhat it does
DashboardTheir to-do (documents returned, queries to answer), reporting line with a callable supervisor number, lead analytics by stage, earnings summary, product quick-launch tiles.
My leadsEvery lead they (and their team) submitted — search, filters, status journey with timestamps, CRM remarks and rejection reasons. Internal CRM remarks never appear here.
Submit leadProduct-specific forms — a Home Loan asks about property and balance transfer, a Business Loan about turnover. PAN/Aadhaar/mobile validated on the spot with plain-language messages.
Apply moreSend an existing customer to another lender — each application tracked separately with its own status.
My teamTheir sub-partners with codes, status and production. Invite someone: the portal generates a private link; the invitee sets their own password and arrives as Pending for QCred to approve. Sub-DSAs invite Connectors; Business/Cluster Heads can invite Sub-DSAs too.
PayoutsCommission earned per case — gross, TDS, net — what’s paid (with UTR) and what’s pending.
LendersThe same Zone → Region → Product catalogue the office uses, minus the payout column. Answers “who will take my customer”.
MarketingPublished creatives as tiles, downloadable; their own download history below.
Products / Notifications / ProfileProduct and offer information · announcements and status alerts (with mark-all-read) · profile across Basic, Bank, Contact, Documents and Password tabs, with a completion checklist — the agreement unlocks only when the profile is complete.
A partner’s first week
  1. Register (or accept an invitation) and set a password.
  2. Complete the profile — personal, business, address, bank — and upload KYC documents. The checklist shows exactly what’s missing.
  3. QCred verifies documents, records training, approves and activates — the partner code is issued.
  4. Submit the first lead from the dashboard tiles, and track it stage by stage.
  5. Rejected document? It shows in their to-do with the reason — replace it in one tap.
Documents are private KYC files live in private storage and are served only through a permission check — a partner can reach their own documents and nobody else’s; a signed-out visitor gets nothing.

Roles handbook

The five-role model

RoleSeesMeant for
Individual ContributorOwn work onlyField staff working their own leads and payouts
SupervisorOwn + everyone mapped under them, any depthTeam leads — sees the whole subtree, approves new joiners in it
Individual Contributor (Central Office)Own work onlyCentral ops processing staff
Supervisor (Central Office)Own + teamCentral ops leads — adds partner approval, document verification, broader tooling
LeaderEverythingLeadership — full visibility and configuration, but not payout release

The earlier roles (Maker, Checker, RM, Manager, National Head…) remain available; the five above are the simplified taxonomy going forward. Assign either from Employees.

Three ideas that make access make sense

ConceptMeaning
PermissionsWhich screens and buttons a role gets — 52 switches, editable in Settings.
Data accessWhose records they see: Own / Team (walks the Reports-to line) / All. Applied inside the database query — out-of-scope records are never sent to the browser.
Tier vs role (partners)The tier (Business Head, Sub-DSA, Connector…) places someone in the network and shapes their code; the role decides what their login can do.

Hard rules that no role overrides

Payout releaseApprove + mark-paid: Checker and Super Admin only. And whoever raises a payout can never approve it.
Partner activationBlocked until training is recorded (§4). Codes are permanent — never reused, even after exit.
Employee entryNew staff cannot sign in until approved by their chain or the corporate office.
Self-protectionYou cannot change your own role or suspend yourself — an administrator can never lock everyone out.
AuditEvery approval, permission change, terms change and payment is recorded with who and when.
