How team visibility works (items 16 & 21)

Think of Reports to as the org chart. A Divisional Manager sees himself + everyone who reports to him + people under them. Example: Priya reports to Ramesh (DM); Amit reports to Priya → Ramesh’s team = Ramesh + Priya + Amit. If Amit’s Reports to is blank, Ramesh never sees Amit’s files — that is expected, not a bug.
