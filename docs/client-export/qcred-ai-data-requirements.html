3The learning model — “based on past data”NEEDS: FILE HISTORY

This is where it becomes AI in the real sense: the model learns which lender actually says yes — per product, profile, geography and ticket size — and starts predicting approval likelihood, not just eligibility. For that it needs your past files with outcomes:

Per past fileWhy it matters
Product · lender submitted to · amount · cityWhat the case was and where it went
Profile (Salaried / SEP / SENP) · CIBIL band · property typeWhat the lender was judging
Outcome — Approved / Rejected / Disbursed / Withdrawn, + rejection reasonThe thing the model learns from
Days to decision · disbursed amountLets it also rank by speed and slippage

No customer names or numbers needed — the model learns lender behaviour, not identities. 6–12 months of files is ideal; even 200–300 rows gives a useful first model, and it improves continuously because every file processed in the CRM from now on records these outcomes automatically. The history you provide is the head start.
