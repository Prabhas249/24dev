[h2] What Makes a Fortune Broiler Bird Different from the Market? 
[p] For a wholesale trader buying live birds, three things matter above everything else: 
[p] 1. Weight uniformity A batch of birds with wide weight variation is harder to sell. Buyers at the processor or retail level want consistent weight — birds that are all close to the same size. Weight uniformity comes from consistent nutrition across the entire cycle and a controlled environment that ensures every bird in the shed gets the same conditions. 
[p] 2. No antibiotic residue risk Processor rejection due to antibiotic residue is a real and growing risk. When a bird’s feed is clean and its environment is healthy, antibiotic use is minimised. When feed has problems and birds get sick, antibiotic use goes up — and residue risk goes with it. 
[p] 3. Reliable supply A trader who builds a supply chain around a bird source needs that source to be reliable — consistent availability, consistent quality, consistent communication. Not a supplier who has birds sometimes and doesn’t sometimes. Fortune addresses all three at the source.
