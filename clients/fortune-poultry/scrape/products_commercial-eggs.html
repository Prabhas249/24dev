Two Problems That Cost Egg Traders Money Every Week.

Traders who buy commercial eggs in bulk deal with two recurring problems that eat directly into profit:

⚠️

Problem 1 — Shell breakage in transit. Broken eggs are lost revenue. You paid for them. You moved them. And they arrive at the buyer as a loss. Shell breakage during transit is almost always a feed problem at the source — layer birds not receiving the right calcium levels produce thin-shelled eggs that cannot survive handling and transport. Calcium alone is not enough. Vitamin D3 must also be present in the feed — without it, the bird cannot absorb and use the calcium it consumes. Many feed formulations include calcium but underdose or skip Vitamin D3. The result is thin shells even when the feed label says calcium is correct.

⚠️

Problem 2 — Drug residue rejection. Eggs rejected at the retailer or processor level due to antibiotic or drug residue are a serious and growing problem in the Haryana egg supply chain. Retailers and institutional buyers are increasingly testing for residues. One rejected batch can damage a trader’s relationship with their buyer. The source of drug residues is almost always the same: layer flocks that are not healthy enough to maintain production without antibiotic support. When the feed is right and the environment is managed, birds stay healthy. When the feed is wrong — unsafe, nutritionally imbalanced, low quality — birds get sick and antibiotic use increases.

Both problems are feed problems. Fortune fixes both at the source.

What Makes Fortune Eggs Different for Your Business.

Fortune’s layer flock eats Fortune’s own lab-tested layer feed — every batch. Here is what that means for the eggs you receive:

Strong shells — by formulation, not by chance

Fortune’s layer feed contains calcium at 3.5 to 4% — the correct range for shell strength in commercial layers. Vitamin D3 is included in every batch to ensure that calcium is actually absorbed and used.

This is not a variable. It is in the formulation. It is in every batch. It is verified in the lab test before the feed reaches the layer flock. The result is consistently strong shells — batch after batch, season after season.

Drug-free production — by design

Fortune’s layer feed formulation is built around gut health through probiotics and digestive enzymes rather than reliance on antibiotics. The full nutritional programme — balanced protein, correct amino acids, safety binders, phytase enzyme — keeps Fortune’s layer flock healthy through proper nutrition.

Healthy birds on correct nutrition do not need antibiotics to maintain production. That is how Fortune keeps drug residue risk low — not through testing at the end, but through a production system that does not generate the problem in the first place.

Supply reliability — 14 years in business

Fortune has operated its Layer Farm continuously since 2011. Three operational units — manufacturing, hatchery, layer farm — running simultaneously. For a trader who needs consistent weekly supply, that operational stability matters.

Fortune’s layer birds eat Fortune’s own lab-tested layer feed — formulated with probiotics and digestive enzymes to maintain gut health without antibiotic dependence.
