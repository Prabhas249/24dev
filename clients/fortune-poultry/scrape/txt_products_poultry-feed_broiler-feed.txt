[h2] Why Do Some Broiler Flocks Take 42 Days When Others Take 35? 
[p] Every broiler farmer in Haryana knows this feeling — same effort, same management, same number of birds. But your batch takes 42 days. Your neighbour’s takes 35. 
[p] The difference is almost always in the feed. Here is what happens when broiler feed has problems: 
[p] Problem 1 — Unsafe feed (aflatoxin) Unsafe feed does not kill your birds quickly. It works slowly. From Day 1, it suppresses their immune system. They use energy to fight off damage instead of growing. By Day 20, you notice something is wrong — but by then, your full investment is already in. 
[p] Research shows that 56% of poultry feed in Haryana has unsafe aflatoxin levels. Feed efficiency drops by up to 10.8% — that is almost one full bag wasted for every 10 you buy. 
[p] Problem 2 — Wrong nutrition for the phase Broiler growth has three distinct phases. Pre-Starter, Starter, and Finisher each need a different protein level and energy level. A single-formula feed — or a formula that gets the ratios wrong — leaves money on the floor every single cycle. 
[p] Problem 3 — Poor pellet quality Broken pellets and feed dust mean your birds physically cannot consume all the nutrition you paid for. Fine dust settles. Birds eat less efficiently. Your FCR suffers. 
[blockquote] Every extra day a broiler bird stays in your shed costs you feed, electricity, medication risk, and market timing. A 7-day delay on 10,000 birds is a serious financial hit.
