[h2] Our Facility — Built for Haryana’s Conditions 
[h3] 🌡️ Environmental Control (EC) Shed 
[p] Haryana’s temperature swings from 5°C in winter to 48°C in summer. Most open sheds cannot handle this. Heat stress alone can drop bird immunity, slow growth, and reduce hatch rates in breeders. 
[p] Fortune’s EC facility solves this with three systems working together: 
[li] Evaporative cooling pads — bring temperature to 18–24°C even in peak summer 
[li] Ammonia exhaust fans — keep ammonia levels below 25 ppm for healthy respiratory function 
[li] Automated light cycles — optimise bird behaviour and growth patterns 
[p] Every bird raised at Fortune has never experienced heat stress from Day 1. That is a real biological advantage — and it shows in the results. 
[h3] 🏗️ 6 Dedicated Grain Godowns 
[p] Most small feed mills store grain in open sheds. Moisture gets in. Temperature swings cause fungal growth. That contamination ends up in your birds’ feed. 
[p] Fortune’s 6 dedicated godowns maintain controlled conditions — temperature, moisture, and aeration — from the day we buy the grain to the day it goes into milling. This is where clean feed starts. Before the factory. Before the lab test. At the source. Our Purpose
