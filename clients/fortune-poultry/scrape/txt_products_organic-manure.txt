[h2] Poultry Manure vs Conventional Farmyard Manure — The Real Difference. 
[p] Poultry manure outperforms conventional farmyard manure on the measures that matter most to a Haryana crop farmer: 
[p] Higher nutrient density 
[p] Poultry manure contains significantly more nitrogen and phosphorus per unit weight than cattle dung. This means you need less quantity to deliver the same nutritional benefit to your soil — reducing transport and application costs. 
[p] Faster nutrient release 
[p] Poultry manure releases its nutrients faster than cattle dung compost. Your crops get access to nitrogen, phosphorus, and potassium when they are in their active growth phases — not weeks after application when the window has passed. 
[p] Better for soil health over time 
[p] The organic matter in poultry manure improves soil structure. Over multiple seasons of application, your soil holds moisture better, resists compaction better, and develops a healthier microbial population. This is the opposite of what chemical fertilizer does to soil over time. 
[p] Cost effective 
[p] Higher nutrient content per kg means lower per-acre application cost compared to the quantity of farmyard manure needed for the same effect. Combined with the reduced need for chemical top-up, poultry manure delivers better return on investment for crop farmers.
