## Section 4: Manufacturing Process

**Headline:** How We Manufacture - Coil to Finished Product

**Sub-Headline:** How raw Jindal-sourced coil becomes a finished, weighbridge-verified pipe, coil, or sheet - start to finish.

**Body:**

Raw stainless steel coil is sourced from Jindal Stainless and processed at our Hisar unit into pipes (round, square, rectangular, oval, slotted, NB tube), coils (including slitting and cut-to-length), and sheets, finished to standard 2B Prime unless a different finish is requested.

Every dispatch is weighed on our Dharam Kanta (weighbridge) before billing, so the invoice reflects actual delivered weight rather than theoretical calculation.

### Step 1: Sourcing

Raw stainless steel coil is sourced directly from Jindal Stainless in 200, 300, and 400 series grades - the same starting material across every product we manufacture.

### Step 2: Cutting & Forming

Coil is processed at our Hisar unit into pipes (round, square, rectangular, oval, slotted, NB tube) through ERW, welded, or OD construction, depending on the product.

### Step 3: Slitting & Cut-to-Length

For coil and sheet orders, material is slit to your required width or cut to your exact length in-house — no separate vendor, no added lead time.

### Step 4: Finishing

Every product is finished to standard 2B Prime unless a different finish is requested, giving a consistent, smooth, industry-standard surface across the batch.

### Step 5: Weighbridge Verification

Before any order leaves our unit, it's weighed on our own Dharam Kanta - so the invoice reflects actual delivered weight, not a theoretical calculation.

### Step 6: Dispatch

Confirmed, weighed, and finished - the order is dispatched ex-works or FOR, based on what you've chosen at confirmation.
