## Section 4: FAQs

**Headline:** Frequently Asked Questions

**Sub-Headline:** The grade questions we get asked most often, industry by industry.

### FAQs 1: Which stainless steel grade is used for dairy and food equipment?

**Answer:** SS 304 is the standard for dairy and food-contact equipment due to its corrosion resistance against moisture and cleaning chemicals.

### FAQs 2: Do you supply stainless steel for heat exchanger tubes?

**Answer:** Yes, message your size and grade requirement on WhatsApp.

### FAQs 3: What stainless steel grade is best for automobile machinery parts?

**Answer:** Depends on exposure - 300-series for corrosion-prone components, 200/400 series for less exposed structural parts.

### FAQs 4: My industry isn't listed here - do you still supply to us?

**Answer:** Yes, this list covers our most common buyer industries, not a limit on who we serve. Message us your application and we'll confirm grade and product fit either way.
