## Section 3: Weight Calculator

**Headline:** Estimate Your Stainless Steel Weight

**Sub-Headline:** The exact formulas we use ourselves, so your estimate matches what actually shows up on the weighbridge, not a generic mild-steel guess.

**Body:** Estimate the weight of stainless steel pipe, coil, or sheet before you order - using correct industry formulas for 300-series SS (density 8.00 g/cm³), not a generic mild-steel shortcut that quietly underestimates weight by close to 2%.

### Formulas

**Hollow pipes/tubes:** Weight (kg/m) = (OD − Wall Thickness) × Wall Thickness × 0.02491 **Flat sheets/plates:** Weight (kg) = Length(m) × Width(m) × Thickness(mm) × Density (8.00 for 300-series, 7.75 for 400-series)

## Section 4: Grade Comparison Tool

**Headline:** Grade Comparison Tool (SS 202 vs 304 vs 316)

**Sub-Headline:** SS 202, 304, and 316 can look identical on the shelf, this table breaks down what actually separates them, so you order the right one the first time.

**Body:** SS 202, 304, and 316 can look identical when new but differ in nickel content, corrosion resistance, and long-term performance, getting it wrong can mean replacing a component within a year or two instead of a decade.

### Grade Comparison Table

| Grade | Nickel Content | Corrosion Resistance | Corrosion Resistance |
|-------|---|---|---|
| SS 202 | Lower | Moderate - can show rust over time in humid/wet conditions | Indoor use, less corrosive-exposure applications, cost-sensitive projects |
| SS 304 | Higher | Strong - handles moisture, cleaning chemicals well | Dairy, food processing, general industrial and outdoor use |
| SS 316 | Highest (with molybdenum) | Strongest - resists chlorides, salt, and harsh chemical exposure | Chemical plants, high-exposure or corrosive-environment applications |

## Section 7: FAQs

**Headline:** Frequently Asked Questions

**Sub-Headline:** The weight and grade questions our buyers ask most, answered in plain language.

### FAQ 1: How do I calculate the weight of a stainless steel pipe?

**Answer:** Weight (kg/m) = (OD − Wall Thickness) × Wall Thickness × 0.02491, using this calculator with your specific OD and wall thickness.

### FAQ 2: What is the density of stainless steel?

**Answer:** Approximately 8.00 g/cm³ for 300-series (like SS 304, 316) and approximately 7.75 g/cm³ for 400-series - using the wrong density (e.g., a generic mild-steel figure) underestimates weight by a meaningful margin.

### FAQ 3: Which is better, SS 202 or SS 304?

**Answer:** Neither is universally "better" - SS 202 is more economical for indoor, lower-exposure use; SS 304 costs more but resists moisture and chemical exposure significantly better over years of use. The right choice depends on your application.

### FAQ 4: Why does my calculated weight not match what I was billed for a past order (from another supplier)?

**Answer:** Standard rolling tolerance means actual thickness, and therefore weight can vary slightly from theoretical calculation; this is industry-wide, governed by standards like IS 1852/ASTM A6, not something specific to one supplier. What matters is whether the supplier weighed and billed you on actual delivered weight, which is what BSSI does on every order.
