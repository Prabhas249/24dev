## Section 2: Content

Bharat Stainless Steel Industries ("BSSI," "we," "us") operates this website. This policy explains, in plain terms, what information we collect when you visit or use this site, and how we use it.

### Information We Collect

When you submit an enquiry through our website form, we collect your name, phone number, product interest, and message. We collect only what's needed to respond to your specific enquiry.

### How We Use Your Information

Used only to route your enquiry via WhatsApp and respond to your product or pricing request. We do not store your details in any email system or backend database beyond what's required to reply, and we do not use your information for unrelated marketing purposes.

### No Email Collection

This website does not collect email addresses at any point. All enquiry communication happens through WhatsApp.

### Cookies & Analytics

This site may use basic analytics tools (e.g., Google Analytics) to understand overall site traffic. These tools may use cookies to collect anonymous usage data. No personally identifiable information is collected through analytics beyond standard, anonymized web traffic data.

### Third-Party Links

Our website may link out to WhatsApp or Google Maps. These third-party services operate under their own privacy policies.

### Data Retention

We retain enquiry information only for as long as needed to respond to and fulfill your request. We do not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties for marketing purposes.

### Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
