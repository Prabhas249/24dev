## Section 10: FAQs

**Headline:** Frequently Asked Questions

**Sub-headline:** What round pipe buyers usually ask before ordering.

### FAQ 1: What is the difference between NB and OD in stainless steel pipes?

**Answer:** NB (Nominal Bore) is an approximate internal-diameter naming convention; OD (Outer Diameter) is the actual measured outer diameter. Buyers sometimes confuse the two, especially from an older drawing, we confirm both measurements before manufacturing to avoid a fitting mismatch on delivery.

### FAQ 2: What is the difference between 200 and 300 series stainless steel?

**Answer:** 300-series (SS 304, SS 316) contains more nickel, giving better corrosion resistance - common in food, pharma, and marine or high-moisture environments. 200-series (SS 202) substitutes some nickel with manganese, more affordable for structural and decorative uses. The grades can look identical when new, which is why some suppliers substitute one for the other without disclosure, we always confirm which grade you're ordering.

### FAQ 3: What is the price of SS 304 round pipe per kg?

**Answer:** Changes daily with the market rate. Message your exact size and quantity on WhatsApp for today's rate, usually within the hour.

### FAQ 4: Do you supply ex-works or with delivery?

**Answer:** Both - ex-works means you arrange freight from our plant; FOR (Free on Road) includes delivery to your site. Tell us your preference when you enquire.

### FAQ 5: Your round pipe looks the same as a cheaper competitor's - what's actually different?

**Answer:** Grade and finish consistency across the batch, and weighbridge-verified weight at dispatch. Two round pipes can look identical when new and behave very differently after a year of use if the grade was substituted or the finish wasn't consistent. Ask us for the grade and finish confirmation in writing before you order, we'll give it.
