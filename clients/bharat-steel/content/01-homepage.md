## Section 2: About Section

### Headline
Who Bharat Stainless Steel Industries Is?

### Sub-Headline
12+ years of manufacturing trust, built order by order in Hisar.

### Body

Bharat Stainless Steel Industries (BSSI) is a stainless steel pipe, coil, and sheet manufacturer based in Hisar, Haryana, supplying 200, 300, and 400 series stainless steel, including SS 202, SS 304, and SS 316, to traders, fabricators, and industrial buyers across Haryana and 210+ cities nationwide.

Since around 2010, Bharat Stainless Steel Industries has sourced its raw material from Jindal Stainless, manufactured pipes (round, square, rectangular, oval, slotted, and NB tube), coils (including slitting and cut-to-length), and sheets in-house at its Hisar unit, and billed every order on actual Dharam Kanta (weighbridge) weight rather than theoretical calculation.

Registered under GST and MSME, BSSI has grown from a local Hisar supplier into a manufacturer serving 650+ customers with 5,500+ MT produced annually - a track record built on consistent grade, honest weight, and fast WhatsApp response, not on claims alone.

## Section 7: Grade & Quality

### Headline
Grade & Quality honesty note

### Sub-Headline
The one honesty check most stainless steel suppliers skip, but the one that protects your project the most.

### Body

The one thing every buyer should know before ordering stainless steel. Not all stainless steel is the same - SS 202 and SS 304 can look identical when new but perform very differently after 1–3 years, especially with moisture exposure. We tell you plainly which grade you're getting and why it fits your use case, before you order.

The same honesty applies to weight: we bill every order on actual weighbridge weight, and small variance from a calculator's theoretical number is normal, expected, and industry-wide due to standard rolling tolerances.
