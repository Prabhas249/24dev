### FAQ 1: What stainless steel grades does BSSI supply?

**Answer:** We supply 200, 300, and 400 series stainless steel across all our pipe, coil, and sheet products. 300-series (SS 304, SS 316) is the standard for food, pharma, and corrosion-prone environments; 200-series (SS 202) is a cost-effective option for structural and decorative work. Still unsure which fits your project? [Use our free Grade Comparison Tool →] or message us your application on WhatsApp.

---

**Answer:** Yes. Coils and sheets are available cut to length as per your requirement, and pipes are supplied from 0.3mm to 8mm thickness and 6mm up to 12 inch size. Share your spec on WhatsApp for same-day confirmation.
