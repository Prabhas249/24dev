## Honesty guardrails (already true in this copy — keep them if you edit)
- "Free hosting for life" = deployed to the client's OWN account, no bills to us. Never imply we host free forever.
- "Free domain" = setup + year 1; renews in their name at cost.
- "Free/starter logo" = starter mark; full custom logo is the ₹7,999 brand kit.
- SEO/AEO = built search-ready & structured for AI — NEVER "guaranteed #1" or "guaranteed cited".
- "Human-crafted", never "AI-free". Never the word "cheap". Never name a website-builder brand (ASCI).
- 24–48h promise is Ad-1 (Starter) only. Ad-2 premium tiers = "a few days", never 24h.
